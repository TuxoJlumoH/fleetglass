# Code of Conduct

Fleetglass adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. The full text at that URL is the binding version and applies to all project
spaces (issues, pull requests, chat, events) and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Summary
We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Expected: empathy and kindness, respect for differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting
constructive feedback, taking responsibility and apologising for mistakes, and focusing on what is
best for the community.

Not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal
or political attacks, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without
explicit permission, and other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement
Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@TuxoJlumoH](https://github.com/TuxoJlumoH), on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers follow the Contributor
Covenant enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
